## Authorization and minimal path

`apps/web/src/app/(authenticated)/drilldown/page.tsx` explicitly refuses `EXPLORATION_LEVEL.DISTRITO` (around line 354), although `apps/web/src/lib/workspace/official-facets.ts` can advertise a district level in available facets. That advertisement does **not** authorize district evidence. Existing `apps/web/src/lib/workspace/official-drilldown-evidence.ts` and the route operate on scoped official evidence; no province-wide result can be inferred by summing a local section or using a polygon. The PBA election district `027` is a partido, whereas national district `02` is the province: preserving the national section `027` is essential.

**Recommendation:** do not ship a filled provincial map, all-partidos list, province total, or district-level route unlock yet. The first truthful vertical path can label Buenos Aires as a *navigation context only* (no implied provincial polygon or metric) and link to the national `(02,027)` exact official section-level `/drilldown` workflow **only when the requested election/category and user authorization actually support it**. The local Coronel Rosales boundary has passed a structural validation only: resolve the WFS reuse terms and election-date correspondence before presenting it as a publishable electoral child. If the UI requires a geographic province anchor rather than a non-geographic context, hold that part until an IGN province polygon is independently fetched, licensed, dated, archived, validated and mapped to the correct identity. Separately verify election/category-specific authorization and coverage before presenting any province-wide figures. Unsupported children and dates must be explicit, not blank zeros or guessed polygons.
