## Sources and reuse

- [IGN GIS layers](https://www.ign.gob.ar/NuestrasActividades/InformacionGeoespacial/CapasSIG) list province polygons; [IGN reuse conditions](https://www.ign.gob.ar/descargas/tyc1.html) require attribution and preserving source date/metadata, and impose conditions on derived and commercial products. No selected PBA province feature, downloadable version, checksum, geometry validation, or 2023/2025 boundary-effective date is recorded. **Province polygon: not accepted.** Do not equate a layer listing to an archived polygon or call it CC BY.
- [PBA/ARBA Partidos catalog](https://catalogo.datos.gba.gob.ar/api/3/action/package_show?id=partidos) declares CC BY 4.0 and reports metadata modified 2026-06-17; that is not 2023/2025 applicability. Its ZIP distribution was not successfully retrieved in the [prior investigation](territorial-source-geometry-terrain-feasibility.md). A separately archived [ARBA WFS `idera:Departamento` GetFeature](https://geo.arba.gov.ar/geoserver/idera/wfs?service=WFS&version=2.0.0&request=GetFeature&typeNames=idera%3ADepartamento&outputFormat=application%2Fjson&srsName=EPSG%3A4326&count=2&CQL_FILTER=cde%3D%2706182%27%20AND%20cca%3D%27113%27&sortBy=cde%20A) covers **only Coronel Rosales**; catalog reuse terms are evidence for the listed Partidos resource, but applicability to this particular WFS response needs explicit confirmation before redistribution.
- [CNE/PBA circuits catalog](https://catalogo.datos.gba.gob.ar/api/3/action/package_show?id=circuitos-electorales) declares CC BY 4.0; archived CNE WFS circuits and electoral-section snapshots exist, but circuits and legislative sections are not a province outline or an accepted all-partidos layer. Their catalog dates and edition-to-election correspondence are unresolved. [DINE results standard v1.0.8](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/sites/default/files/preservacionresultadoselectorales_1.0.8.pdf) distinguishes provincial district, partido-level section, provincial legislative section, circuit, establishment and mesa; IDs are election-scoped. [DINE results portal](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/dine/resultados-electorales) publishes provisional results, not proof of complete definitive provincial ingestion. [PBA 2025 definitive municipal result example](https://www.juntaelectoral.gba.gov.ar/escrutinio-definitivo-2025/distrito_027.html) is a local result, not a province-wide denominator.

## Authorization and minimal path

`apps/web/src/app/(authenticated)/drilldown/page.tsx` explicitly refuses `EXPLORATION_LEVEL.DISTRITO` (around line 354), although `apps/web/src/lib/workspace/official-facets.ts` can advertise a district level in available facets. That advertisement does **not** authorize district evidence. Existing `apps/web/src/lib/workspace/official-drilldown-evidence.ts` and the route operate on scoped official evidence; no province-wide result can be inferred by summing a local section or using a polygon. The PBA election district `027` is a partido, whereas national district `02` is the province: preserving the national section `027` is essential.

**Recommendation:** do not ship a filled provincial map, all-partidos list, province total, or district-level route unlock yet. The first truthful vertical path can label Buenos Aires as a *navigation context only* (no implied provincial polygon or metric) and link to the national `(02,027)` exact official section-level `/drilldown` workflow **only when the requested election/category and user authorization actually support it**. If the UI requires a geographic province anchor rather than a non-geographic context, hold that part until an IGN province polygon is independently fetched, licensed, dated, archived, validated and mapped to the correct identity. Separately verify election/category-specific authorization and coverage before presenting any province-wide figures. Unsupported children and dates must be explicit, not blank zeros or guessed polygons.
