# Territorial Electoral Intelligence

Shared electoral vocabulary for Votus.

## Language

**Territorial intelligence**: Evidence-bound understanding of electoral support and its changes across territories, with explicit uncertainty and limits.
_Avoid_: Deterministic prediction.

**Territory**: A geographically situated area to which an electoral observation or question refers; its identity includes the applicable jurisdiction scheme.

**Electoral granularity**: The finest unit at which electoral evidence distinguishes results, such as a circuit, establishment or mesa.

**Geographic granularity**: The finest unit whose location or boundary is supported by geographic evidence; it need not match electoral granularity.

**Cartographable territory**: A territory with sufficient source-backed geographic evidence to represent its location or extent truthfully at the declared precision.

**Non-geographic sublayout**: An arrangement of electoral units anchored to a known place without claiming their positions, spacing or shapes are real geography.

**Canonical party/alliance**: The curated political identity used to relate electoral observations; it is distinct from an election's ballot label and does not imply continuity without evidence.

**Electoral label**: The party, alliance or list name appearing in a particular election's evidence.
_Avoid_: Treating a ballot label as a permanent political identity.

**Territorial dominance**: The leading party/alliance within a declared territory, election and vote basis; a tie or insufficient evidence does not establish a unique winner.

**Physical terrain**: The real elevation and relief of the land, independent of electoral results.

**Electoral extrusion**: Visual height representing a declared electoral metric, not physical elevation.

**Fiscalización**: Internal election-observation evidence and operational coverage, distinct from official results and drawn from a non-random sample with an explicit coverage denominator.

**Simulación**: A hypothetical electoral or seat-allocation scenario governed by stated inputs, assumptions and applicable statutory rules, not a forecast.

**Análisis/Prospectiva**: Probabilistic interpretation of electoral evidence and possible futures, with explicit assumptions, confidence and limitations; recommendations are conditional, not deterministic predictions.
